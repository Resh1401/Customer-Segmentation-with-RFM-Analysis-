# Customer Segmentation with RFM Analysis

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cd3ef638-441e-4d04-b2bb-fb5487e04c5b)

1. Project Objectives

* Analyze customer purchasing behavior over time
* Segment customers using RFM scores to identify high-value and at-risk groups
* Recommend personalized marketing strategies to boost sales and customer retention
* Uncover sales trends across weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual periods for strategic planning

2. Key Highlights
   
* Conducted comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis to identify patterns in sales across time and product categories
* Built an RFM model in KNIME and segmented customers into strategic groups like Best, Loyal, Lost, and At-Risk
* Identified top 5 customers in each category, enabling the business to tailor retention, reward, or re-engagement strategies
* Created a clear customer targeting framework with visual insights for marketing and sales teams
* Final segmentation helped the company identify underserved customers and high-potential segments
